# References and further study

- Casella, G., and Berger, R. L. *Statistical Inference*, 2nd ed. Duxbury, 2002. Chapters on likelihood and point estimation.
- Rice, J. A. *Mathematical Statistics and Data Analysis*, 3rd ed. Cengage, 2006. Clear development of likelihood and asymptotic methods.
- Wasserman, L. *All of Statistics*. Springer, 2004. Concise reference for maximum likelihood and inference.
- NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook of Statistical Methods, "Maximum Likelihood Estimation." https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/ (accessed 2025-02-14).
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, "Bayesian Epistemology," for conceptual distinctions between likelihood and posterior probability. https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/epistemology-bayesian/ (accessed 2025-02-14).
